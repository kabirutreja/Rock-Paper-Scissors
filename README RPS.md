
# Rock-Paper-Scissor

A first-person Rock-Paper-Scissor combat game where you outsmart enemies and clear the arena.

## Controls

**Action      ==> Key**

Move  ==> WASD

Look around ==> Mouse

Interact ==> E

Deploy ability ==> space



## Gameplay Basics
**Rock-paper-scissor combat :** Enemies and Player units carry a type -- Rock,paper,scissor. When two different types collide, the standard RPS rule decides who's destroyed. Same-type collisions hae no effect.

**Boxes :** Destructible boxes respawn after a set delay, with a particle effect and sound cue, Boxes won't respawn if the player is standing too close. They provide one of these powerup at random - Rock,paper,scissor.

**Scoring :** Defeating an enemy increases your score.

**Game Over :** Colliding with an enemy as the player ends the run.
